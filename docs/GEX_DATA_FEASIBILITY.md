## Quant Data field contract: what the guide does and does not establish

The official guide documents `POST /v1/options/tool/exposure-by-strike`, Bearer authentication, and `representationMode` values `PER_ONE_PERCENT_MOVE`, `PER_ONE_DOLLAR_MOVE`, `RAW`. Under percent mode, `exposureMap[expiry][strike]` holds **dealer-signed dollar exposure per 1% underlying move**: `callExposure` positive, `putExposure` negative; an omitted side can mean zero. Do **not** negate puts a second time. `stockPrice` is the session spot. `POST /v1/options/tool/interval-map` returns epoch-millisecond keys → expiry → strike → `CALL`/`PUT`, approximately 405 minute buckets per SPY session. A minute bucket is not proof of original receipt/publication time. The guide advertises 365+ day history and 240 requests/minute, not a tested oldest date or guaranteed latency. Its $149.99 monthly/$124.99 monthly billed-annually figures were stated as checked June 30; current total cost/rights require confirmation. No documented portfolio-repricing flip field/method was verified; derive neither a portfolio root nor a dealer inventory claim from strike signs alone. [Quant Data official guide](https://help.quantdata.us/en/articles/15807345-gamma-exposure-gex-api-python-quickstart-dealer-positioning-guide).

The guide is an advertised analytical contract, not independent validation of actual dealer positions. Its illustrative API responses are publisher examples, not project-acquired evidence. An aggregate exposure map lacks the contract-level IV/OI inputs needed to reproduce a portfolio-repriced root unless separately supplied. No field with units/counts missing, including screenshot B's `129473`, may be relabeled GEX because it looks like the map.

## Massive snapshot and Intrinio EOD are not intraday history substitutes

Massive chain OI is explicitly the quantity held at the end of the previous trading day; Greeks can be absent, including deep-ITM circumstances. Snapshot quote/trade availability depends on the plan, and underlying data depends on a separate stock plan. [Chain semantics](https://massive.com/docs/rest/options/snapshots/option-chain-snapshot). Historical quotes expose nanosecond SIP receive timestamps. They do not by themselves include historical Greek estimates, historical OI, provider publication clocks or our receipt clocks. [Quote schema](https://massive.com/docs/rest/options/trades-quotes/quotes). A historical contract-list `as_of` filter, if used, must not be presented as an as-of snapshot.

Intrinio's EOD product includes close bid/ask, volume, Greeks, OI and next-day OI. Even if the price is labeled with the trading date, receipt after market close or the next morning places those observations outside an earlier intraday decision's information set. [EOD fields and timing](https://intrinio.com/options/eod-historical-options). Its intraday product is separately enterprise-gated, with interval-only history from December 8, 2022 and materially different formats. One page's real-time or EOD entitlement does not establish another product's historical Greek archive. [Intraday formats](https://intrinio.com/options/intraday-historical-options).

## Provider sample checklist and acceptance record

Before granting D0, require a sanitized evidence receipt for each probe: request/response status, bounded selection, actual first/last timestamps, pagination completion, chain/expiry coverage, missing fields, checksum, schema version, retrieval timestamp, provider/feed and declared latency. Hash raw files privately; publish schemas and synthetic equivalents only if rights prohibit sample publication.

- **Identity and units:** native underlying, root, contract type, strike, actual expiry/last-trading/settlement convention, multiplier/deliverable adjustments; exposure metric and currency, per-dollar/per-percent/raw scale, signed-vendor versus unsigned model, IV/gamma definition and model version.
- **Point in time:** economic/event date and timezone, original publication/availability versus current retrieval, our receipt timestamp, revisions/corrections, OI reference date/publication, quote/underlying age, chain completeness. Unknown publication clocks remain unknown; `received_now` cannot repair historical availability.
- **Level semantics:** snapshot cell reconciliation to expiry/strike totals; which missing sides truly mean zero; provider wall definitions; portfolio-repriced roots versus per-strike crossings; zero/multiple roots and stability assumptions. Precomputed maps without repricing inputs do not pass a reproducible portfolio-root test.
- **Rights and cost:** explicit lawful local storage/retention, caching, backup, derived-data publication, screenshot/dashboard distribution, personal/commercial use, OPRA/index entitlements and any exchange agreement. Current total charge, limits and renewal terms are unknown until confirmed. No public page or existing equity subscription grants these rights by inference.
- **Live readiness:** authorized feed, actual complete session sampling outside sealed-outcome research, observed end-to-end delay, gaps, reconnect, out-of-order/revision handling, rate limits and stale fail-closed behavior. An advertised cadence or vendor server receipt timestamp is not measured application latency.
